# Issue Template

**Purpose**: Sent to clients when they report a problem, so the report captures the device, environment, and reproducible steps needed to diagnose it.

---

## Template

### About your device

- **Type of device** — PC, laptop, tablet, phone, TV, console, etc.
- **Operating system** — Windows, macOS, Android, iOS, Linux, etc.
- **Environment** — Browser plus version (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge, etc.) or app plus version.

### About the issue

- **Expected** — What you expected to happen.
- **Actual** — What actually happened.
- **Steps to reproduce** — The exact steps required to trigger the problem.

---

_A structured report beats a vague "it's broken" every time and saves a back-and-forth round trip._
