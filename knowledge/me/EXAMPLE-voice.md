# Voice (Example)

> **Template.** Copy this file to `voice.md` and replace everything below.
> Keep it under ~500 words — a voice file nobody finishes reading protects nothing.
> Delete this blockquote.

Two registers. Pick by artifact, never mix.

## Working voice (agent sessions, internal notes)

Describe how you want agents to talk to you: terse or full prose, fragments allowed or not,
what to drop (filler, pleasantries, hedging), what must always stay (technical substance,
caveats, security warnings). State whether agents should adopt it by default or only on request,
and what is always written normally (code, commits, client-facing text).

## Public/business voice (site copy, posts, outreach, client email)

Describe your house style as observable traits, not adjectives:

- Sentence length, concreteness, how you handle numbers
- How you talk about tradeoffs and competitors
- Wit: allowed, when, at whose expense (never the client's)
- Pronoun policy — "we" vs "I", and when each applies
- Sell pressure — offer then stop, or something else

## Banned

List the words and phrases that never appear in your output. Two useful groups:

- **AI-isms**: (e.g. delve, seamless, elevate, unlock, robust, "it's important to note")
- **Your industry's fluff**: (e.g. "solutions provider", "passionate about")

Also list banned *patterns* if any: em-dash chains, rule-of-three padding, "not just X, but Y".

## Hits

2-3 short quotes from your real output that nail the voice. One line each on why it works.

## Miss

One example that fails — ideally a real draft you rejected, or a deliberate rewrite of a hit
into slop. Explain why it misses; the miss teaches more than the hits.
